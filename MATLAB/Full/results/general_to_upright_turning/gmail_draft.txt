The essay "Steering Control of an Autonomous Unicycle" classifies several steady motions of a rolling wheel with a moving point mass under zero input to the mass. In our full-model simulation, which allows a steady actuator force, I can first move the system onto a general turning-rolling manifold and then reach a zero-lean turning equilibrium. I also tried to reach the essay's nontilted turning state, where F = 0. The current controller did not converge to the specific mass position and yaw rate at which the inertial effects balance and the required F vanishes; the zero-lean state it reaches still needs a nonzero F.

The lean angle reaches zero, but the yaw rate remains nonzero, so this does not yet return the system to straight rolling. I suspect this may be close to the limit of the current manifold approach. I am also concerned that selecting the target through the local invariant could make the method sensitive to sensor noise and bias, although I have not tested that yet. Do you have any suggestions for next steps? I have attached plots of the two stages.

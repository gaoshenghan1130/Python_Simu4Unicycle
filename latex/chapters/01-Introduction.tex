\chapter{Introduction}
\label{chap:introduction}

After integrating the linear motor, we developed a theoretically functional unicycle robot capable of balancing itself and moving forward at a desired speed. However, several challenges remain before the system can operate reliably in practice. This report documents how these challenges emerged and how we attempted to address them. It also discusses why some problems may be fundamentally difficult to resolve within the current design and explains why balancing a unicycle robot using this type of linear motor is particularly challenging.
